Fire Dashboard Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Overview

Fire Dashboard is a personal dashboard that displays information such as calendar events and weather. Your privacy is important.

Google Account Information

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, the dashboard requests read-only access to your primary Google Calendar events.

Calendar information is retrieved through a private server operated using Cloudflare Workers. The server uses an access token to retrieve events from Google and returns event information to the dashboard.

Storage and Security

Google refresh tokens are stored in a restricted Cloudflare Workers KV namespace. Temporary login sessions are also stored there. The dashboard stores a session token in the browser's local storage to maintain your login.

Reasonable measures are used to protect this information. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Sharing

Google Calendar information is not intentionally sold, shared with advertisers, or made publicly available. It is only used to provide calendar functionality to the authorized user.

Data Retention and Deletion

The dashboard retains authentication information as needed to provide its functionality. You can revoke the dashboard's Google Account access through your Google Account security settings. Stored authentication information can also be deleted from the server.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the project administrator using the support email listed on the Google consent screen.
